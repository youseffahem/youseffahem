<table>
<tr>
<td width="66%" valign="top">

<p>A server-rendered property management platform that centralizes listings, agent assignments and customer inquiries in one normalized relational database. Consistency is enforced at the database layer through foreign keys, cascade rules and constraints rather than by convention.</p>

<ul>
<li><b>Six normalized InnoDB tables</b> with <code>RESTRICT</code>, <code>SET NULL</code> and <code>CASCADE</code> delete behaviour, <code>CHECK</code> constraints, <code>ENUM</code> domains and <code>DECIMAL(14,2)</code> pricing</li>
<li><b>Thin-controller architecture</b> &mdash; routes delegate to paired <code>*_validation</code> and <code>*_queries</code> modules, so validation is testable without a database</li>
<li><b>Live analytics dashboard</b> computing twelve KPIs and five charts from aggregate SQL, drawn with CSS <code>conic-gradient</code> and no charting library</li>
<li><b>Image galleries</b> of up to twelve ordered images per property, uploads validated by extension and stored under UUID filenames</li>
<li><b>Lead pipeline</b> with a controlled <code>New</code> to <code>Contacted</code> to <code>Closed</code> status progression</li>
<li><b>274 passing tests</b> across API, validation, analytics and schema, including DDL introspection that attempts real constraint violations against MySQL</li>
<li><b>Documented security model</b> &mdash; <code>%s</code> parameter binding throughout, Jinja2 autoescaping with no <code>safe</code> or <code>Markup</code> calls, POST-only deletes, and blocked <code>javascript:</code> and <code>data:</code> URLs</li>
<li><b>Accessibility</b> &mdash; skip link, semantic landmarks, keyboard-operable gallery and full <code>prefers-reduced-motion</code> support</li>
</ul>

<td width="66%" valign="top">

<p>An end-to-end machine learning system for cardiovascular risk assessment, built on the UCI Heart Disease dataset and delivered through an interactive web interface.</p>

<ul>
<li><b>303 patient records, 13 clinical features</b>, binary classification across roughly 54 percent positive and 46 percent negative cases</li>
<li><b>Four models compared</b> &mdash; Random Forest, XGBoost, SVM and Logistic Regression, evaluated on accuracy, F1, ROC-AUC, precision and recall</li>

<li><b>Feature selection</b> through recursive feature elimination and statistical testing, followed by PCA for dimensionality reduction</li>
<li><b>Grid and randomized hyperparameter search</b> with stratified cross-validation and SMOTE oversampling</li>
<li><b>Top predictive features</b> identified as chest pain type, maximum heart rate, ST depression, major vessel count and thalassemia type</li>
<li><b>Streamlit deployment</b> with Plotly visualisations, containerized with Docker and wired to GitHub Actions</li>
